# Open Blockers

Classification remains **Not Ready** until these are resolved or explicitly excluded from a narrower, approved scope.

## P0

- No isolated PostgreSQL target for migration, read/write, cross-patient, cross-facility, cross-jurisdiction and transactional lifecycle proof.
- No LiveKit/TURN credentials or two-/three-browser media-plane, relay, reconnect, low-bandwidth and mobile evidence.
- No backup/restore drill, production-mode startup soak, monitoring/alert proof or deployed-SHA verification.
- Government invitation/approval lifecycle and immediate revocation require full browser/database verification; this branch adds MFA and fail-closed scope enforcement but does not prove end to end provisioning.
- Messaging, EMR, signed SOAP, export, refill and pharmacy ownership need authenticated database/browser attack tests.
- Security review is incomplete for CSRF, upload abuse, CSP, provider webhooks, AI data flows and sensitive logging across the entire monorepo.

## P1

- Government existing-data intake is missing: no complete source registry, CSV/XLSX/JSON import, mappings, quarantine, duplicate prevention, maker-checker approval, commit, reconciliation or rollback.
- Non-technical scoped search, recent searches, saved views and filtered export are missing.
- Indicator metadata/versioning does not yet cover the complete Board-approved catalogue, definitions, numerators, denominators, targets, authority and effective versions.
- DHIS2 contract server, retry/backoff/idempotency/reconciliation tests and authorized sandbox evidence are absent.
- Executive/HMIS dashboards require approved definitions, quality metadata, drilldown proof and government UAT.
- AI clinical/government grounding, citation, prompt-injection, PHI-provider authorization, safety and hallucination evaluation packs are absent.
- Pharmacy inventory import, live interaction checking, settlement/webhook and referral financial simulations are incomplete.
- Full desktop/mobile, WCAG AA, keyboard, reduced-motion and performance evidence is absent.

## External prerequisites

- Authorized fictional/de-identified staging data and PostgreSQL environment.
- LiveKit and TURN validation environment.
- Authorized DHIS2 sandbox, approved mappings, organization units and data-sharing/government approvals.
- Payment/communication/provider sandbox credentials for every enabled integration.
- PHCB/hospital policy decisions for ownership, access, retention, legal hold, incident response, offboarding and indicator approval.

Technical completion must not be interpreted as NDPR, legal, clinical or government approval.
