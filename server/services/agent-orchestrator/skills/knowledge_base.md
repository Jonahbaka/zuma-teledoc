# DoctaRx Knowledge Base (Public)

## What is DoctaRx?
DoctaRx is a HIPAA-compliant telehealth and e-prescribing platform that connects patients with board-certified healthcare providers through secure video consultations. Built for the modern healthcare experience, DoctaRx eliminates waiting rooms and geographic barriers.

## How It Works
1. Create your account at doctarx.com/patient/register
2. Describe your symptoms or select a reason for your visit
3. Our AI triage system assesses urgency and matches you with the right provider
4. Join a secure video consultation from any device with a camera
5. Receive prescriptions electronically sent to your preferred pharmacy
6. Access your records, lab results, and care plans anytime

## Features

### For Patients
- Secure video consultations with licensed providers
- AI-powered symptom triage for faster care
- Electronic prescriptions sent directly to your pharmacy
- Digital insurance wallet with eligibility verification
- Complete medical records accessible from your dashboard
- Secure messaging with your care team
- Medication management and refill tracking

### For Providers
- Full clinical encounter management (Mini-EHR)
- AI Clinical Co-Pilot for real-time decision support
- SOAP note drafting with AI assistance
- e-Prescribing with drug interaction checks
- Patient panel management
- Credentialing and license verification
- Revenue analytics and practice insights

### For Organizations
- Multi-provider practice management
- HIPAA-compliant infrastructure
- Analytics and population health tools
- CRM and patient outreach
- Integration with existing EHR systems via FHIR

## Emergency Notice
DoctaRx is not an emergency service. If you are experiencing a life-threatening emergency, call 911 immediately. Do not use this platform for emergencies including chest pain, stroke symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, or active hemorrhage.
