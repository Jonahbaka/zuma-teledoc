## Art Direction Memo For Engineering And Design

Use these images as a single branded system, not as isolated decorations. Keep cropping consistent: faces should feel close enough for trust, devices should be readable enough to explain the product, and backgrounds should carry local context without clutter. Do not place heavy gradients, large text blocks, or badges directly on top of faces or devices. Use subtle overlays only when contrast is required.

For Nigeria, preserve Abuja/Lagos cues, mobile-first device use, local payment and pharmacy coordination context, and realistic outpatient care. For the United States, preserve HIPAA-ready professionalism, home convenience, pharmacy routing, and family follow-up. Both markets should share the same premium health-tech photography language: warm daylight, natural skin, restrained emerald-teal accents, clean composition, and believable human moments.

If editing existing images, keep the original role and composition where possible. Replace only the weak parts: lighting, color grade, unrealistic faces or hands, generic setting, poor device UI, or untrustworthy pharmacy handling. If generating from scratch, match the suggested filename and aspect ratio so the frontend can swap assets without layout drift.
