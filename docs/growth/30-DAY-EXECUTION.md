## Measure the loop without collecting health data

Use campaign IDs 01–12 to compare public visits, CTA clicks, business enquiries, qualified clinic discussions and agreed pilots. UTM links alone do not implement analytics or prove attribution: confirm that the deployed analytics actually capture these fields before reporting campaign conversion.

Keep an aggregate weekly sheet with: campaign ID, publication URL/date, public visits when available, business enquiries, qualified discussions, demos completed, pilots agreed, cost and known attribution limitations. No symptom text, diagnosis, appointment identifiers, voice transcripts, prescription data or patient names belong in this sheet.

Report actual counts, including zero. The target is a useful repeatable acquisition loop; no reach, customer or revenue target is represented as achieved. Optimize for relevant enquiries and successful onboarding, not impressions alone.
