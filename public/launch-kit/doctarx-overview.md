# DoctaRx

Connected care, from the first conversation to the next step.

DoctaRx is a healthcare software platform bringing patient and provider experiences together across appointments, voice, video, pharmacy coordination, referrals and records. Its goal is to make the work around a consultation easier to follow for everyone involved.

Explore the platform: https://doctarx.com

Provider and partnership enquiries: https://doctarx.com/contact

International information: https://doctarx.com/international

## What we are building toward

- A clearer path from an enquiry to an appointment and follow-up.
- Live voice with speech-to-text and text-to-speech fallback.
- Connected workflows for patients, providers and care partners.
- A public experience that people in different markets can understand.

Clinical voice validation and real-device testing remain work in progress. Service availability depends on location, provider participation and the applicable requirements. International information pages do not mean clinical services are available in every country.
