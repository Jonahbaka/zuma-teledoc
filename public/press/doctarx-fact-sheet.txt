SHORT DESCRIPTION
DoctaRx is a connected virtual care and clinical operations platform bringing patient access, provider workflows and care coordination into one product.

PRODUCT OVERVIEW
DoctaRx brings together patient and provider experiences for appointments, video visits, records, prescriptions, pharmacy coordination, referrals and lab handoffs. Its voice experience includes live voice with speech-to-text and text-to-speech fallback. The product's direction is connected care across languages and local health systems. Clinical voice validation remains in progress, and service availability depends on location, participating providers and configuration.

DIRECTION
DoctaRx is building around a practical idea: make the experience useful for care teams, and make it easier for patients to stay connected to their care. Its ambition is connected care across languages and local health systems.
