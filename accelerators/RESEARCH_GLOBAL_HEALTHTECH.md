# Global HealthTech & Telemedicine Accelerators -- Detailed Research

> Research compiled February 20, 2026

---

## TIER 1: HIGHEST FIT -- Traditional Accelerators with Direct Investment

### 1. Y Combinator (YC)
- **Website:** https://www.ycombinator.com/
- **Deadline:** Spring 2026 on-time was Feb 9; late apps still accepted. Summer 2026 ~May 2026.
- **Duration:** 3 months, in-person San Francisco
- **Investment:** $500K ($125K for 7% + $375K uncapped SAFE with MFN)
- **Fit:** 95+ digital health startups funded, 144+ healthtech companies. Alumni include telehealth companies (Ankr, Reviving Mind, Beacon Health, Nourish). ~1.5-2% acceptance rate.

### 2. Techstars AI Health Baltimore
- **Website:** https://www.techstars.com/accelerators/baltimore-ai-health
- **Deadline:** Fall 2026 apps open Mar 2; deadline Jun 10, 2026
- **Duration:** 13 weeks, in-person Baltimore
- **Investment:** $220K ($20K for 5% + $200K SAFE)
- **Partners:** Johns Hopkins University + CareFirst BlueCross BlueShield
- **Fit:** Specifically AI-driven healthcare; clinical validation through Johns Hopkins

### 3. Cedars-Sinai Accelerator
- **Website:** https://csaccelerator.com/
- **Deadline:** Typically March 15 annually
- **Duration:** 3 months, Los Angeles (hybrid available)
- **Investment:** $100K per company
- **Fit:** 300+ clinician/exec mentors; alumni raised $800M+; clinical workflow tools

### 4. Health Wildcatters (Dallas, TX)
- **Website:** https://www.healthwildcatters.com/accelerator
- **Deadline:** Historically spring; program runs Sep-Nov
- **Duration:** 12 weeks, hybrid
- **Investment:** $30K + $200K+ value for 8% equity; follow-on up to $380K
- **Fit:** Early-stage health IT/SaaS/digital health; international welcome

## TIER 2: Corporate-Connected & Enterprise-Focused

### 5. Dreamit HealthTech
- **Website:** https://www.dreamit.com/healthtech
- **Deadline:** Rolling; Customer Sprints 4x/year
- **Duration:** 14 weeks with 2-week Customer/Investor Sprints
- **Investment:** $25K-$1M depending on stage
- **Fit:** Enterprise customer pipeline; pitch to 15-25 health system execs

### 6. Plug and Play Health
- **Website:** https://www.plugandplaytechcenter.com/
- **Deadline:** Rolling; 2 cohorts/year of 20 startups each
- **Duration:** 3 months, remote-first
- **Investment:** NO equity required; optional $50K-$250K (1-5%)
- **Partners:** Cleveland Clinic, J&J, Sanofi, Roche

### 7. TMC Innovation HealthTech (Houston, TX)
- **Website:** https://www.tmc.edu/innovation/accelerator-healthtech/
- **Deadline:** ~May annually; 19th cohort started Nov 2025
- **Duration:** 6 months, in-person Houston
- **Investment:** No equity; TMC Venture Fund ($50M) invests separately

## TIER 3: Membership & Equity-Free Models

### 8. StartUp Health
- **Website:** https://www.startuphealth.com/
- **Application:** Rolling
- **Model:** Lifetime membership; $100K/quarter via Moonshots Impact Rolling Fund
- **Portfolio:** 380+ companies, 26 countries, $25B collective valuation

### 9. MATTER (Chicago)
- **Website:** https://matter.health/
- **Model:** Non-profit, zero-equity membership
- **Network:** 800+ startups; BARDA Accelerator Network Hub

### 10. MassChallenge HealthTech
- **Website:** https://masschallenge.org/healthcare-and-life-sciences/
- **Model:** Completely equity-free, zero-cost
- **Duration:** 4 months; Switzerland cohort deadline was Mar 4, 2026
- **Alumni:** Raised $16B+, created 77,000 jobs
