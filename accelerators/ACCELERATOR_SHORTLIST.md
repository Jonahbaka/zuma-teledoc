## Key Strengths to Emphasize in All Applications

1. **HIPAA Compliance** -- AES-256-GCM encryption, audit logging, access controls. Most early-stage telehealth startups lack this regulatory maturity.
2. **AI Triage (Nurse Nova)** -- 62% of digital health funding in H1 2025 went to AI-powered companies. This is the #1 trend accelerators want.
3. **Multi-Portal Architecture** -- Patient/Provider/Admin portals demonstrate enterprise readiness and B2B potential.
4. **Production-Ready Platform** -- Not just a prototype. Video consultations, encrypted messaging, SOAP notes, subscription billing all working.
5. **Modern Tech Stack** -- React/Next.js 15, Express, PostgreSQL signals engineering quality and scalability.
6. **Social Impact** -- Addresses healthcare access gaps in underserved markets (Africa: 1:5,000 doctor-patient ratio; LatAm: massive telemedicine adoption post-COVID).
7. **Subscription Revenue Model** -- Recurring SaaS revenue (Free/Gold/Platinum tiers) is attractive to investors.
