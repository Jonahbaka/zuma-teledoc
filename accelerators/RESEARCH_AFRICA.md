# Africa-Focused Accelerators & Incubators -- Detailed Research

> Research compiled February 20, 2026

---

## CURRENTLY ACCEPTING APPLICATIONS (2026)

### 1. Google for Startups Accelerator: Africa (10th Cohort)
- **Website:** https://startup.google.com/programs/accelerator/africa/
- **Deadline:** March 18, 2026
- **Duration:** 12 weeks (Apr-Jun 2026), hybrid
- **Investment:** Equity-free. Up to $350K Google Cloud credits
- **Focus:** AI-first; growth-stage African startups
- **Fit:** STRONG -- AI triage aligns with "AI First" theme; 180+ alumni raised $350M+

### 2. Tony Elumelu Foundation (TEEP) 2026
- **Website:** https://www.tonyelumelufoundation.org/
- **Deadline:** March 1, 2026
- **Investment:** $5K non-refundable seed (equity-free)
- **Focus:** All sectors; African entrepreneurs aged 18+; businesses 0-5 years
- **Fit:** MODERATE -- modest capital but 24,000+ alumni network

### 3. Katapult Africa Accelerator 2026
- **Website:** https://katapult.vc/africa/accelerator/
- **Deadline:** April 25, 2026 (rolling review)
- **Duration:** 90 days, fully digital
- **Investment:** $150K-$500K equity investment
- **Focus:** Climate/agriculture primarily; LOW-MODERATE fit unless healthcare framed as essential services

## ROLLING / ALWAYS-OPEN APPLICATIONS

### 4. Flat6Labs Africa Seed Fund
- **Website:** https://flat6labs.com/
- **Investment:** $150K-$500K (pre-seed to pre-Series A); $95M fund backed by GIZ/IFC
- **Location:** Cairo, Tunis, expanding to Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya
- **Focus:** Healthtech explicitly listed as target sector
- **Fit:** STRONG

### 5. Founders Factory Africa (FFA)
- **Website:** https://www.foundersfactory.africa/
- **Duration:** 6-month bespoke acceleration
- **Location:** Johannesburg; pan-African (11 countries)
- **Partners:** Standard Bank
- **Fit:** STRONG -- 55+ startups supported

### 6. CcHub (Co-Creation Hub)
- **Website:** https://cchub.africa/
- **Duration:** 12-week programs
- **Location:** Lagos + Nairobi (via iHub)
- **Investment:** Up to $100K equity-free
- **Fit:** STRONG -- invested in OneHealth (telehealth); runs ADEIN healthtech challenges

### 7. Savannah Fund
- **Website:** https://savannah.vc/
- **Location:** Nairobi, Kenya
- **Investment:** $25K accelerator + follow-on up to $1M; 12% equity
- **Fit:** MODERATE -- formal cohorts may be paused but seed fund active

### 8. Pangea Accelerator
- **Website:** https://pangeaa.com/
- **Duration:** 3-month milestone-based
- **Location:** Kenya (Kenyan-Norwegian venture builder)
- **Investment:** Up to ~$300K+ at Demo Day
- **Focus:** Health explicitly listed; requires 50% women on team
- **Fit:** MODERATE-STRONG

## WATCH LIST (Application Windows Expected Later 2026)

### 9. HealthTech Hub Africa (HTHA) -- STRONGEST FIT
- **Website:** https://thehealthtech.org/
- **Duration:** 8 months from Kigali (Norrsken House)
- **Focus:** EXCLUSIVELY healthtech -- Virtual Health, AI diagnostics
- **Requirements:** African-registered; 2+ team; post-revenue; 1+ year operating
- **Why strongest:** Virtual Health category is tailor-made for telehealth; teaches HL7 FHIR interoperability

### 10. Villgro Africa
- **Website:** https://villgroafrica.org/
- **Duration:** 6-8 months from Nairobi
- **Investment:** $100K-$250K catalytic seed
- **Focus:** Healthcare businesses in Africa

### 11. UNDP timbuktoo HealthTech Accelerator
- **Website:** https://www.undp.org/africa/projects/timbuktoo
- **Duration:** 6 months from Kigali (2-week bootcamp + 5-month incubation)
- **Investment:** Equity-free grants up to $100K
- **Focus:** AI diagnostics, telemedicine, biotech; part of $1B initiative
- **Fit:** VERY STRONG -- 957 applicants, 40 selected for first cohort

### 12. Startupbootcamp AfriTech
- **Website:** https://startupbootcamp.org/accelerator/afritech
- **Duration:** 3 months in Cape Town/Johannesburg
- **Investment:** EUR 15K + mentors; 1% acceptance rate
- **Focus:** Fintech primarily, but includes Digital Health

### 13. GSMA Innovation Fund
- **Website:** https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/gsma-innovation-fund/
- **Duration:** 12-18 months
- **Investment:** Grants; funded by UK FCDO and Swedish Sida
- **Focus:** Mobile-enabled health solutions for LMICs

### 14. Accelerate Africa (Future Africa / Prosper Africa)
- **Website:** https://www.acceler8.africa/
- **Duration:** 8 weeks; Cohort 5 expected mid-2026
- **Investment:** Equity-free; optional $250K-$500K post-program from Future Africa fund

### 15. Afreximbank Accelerator
- **Website:** https://accelerator-programme.afreximbank.com/
- **Focus:** Trade innovation -- LOW fit unless cross-border telehealth

### 16. GreenHouse Capital (Lagos)
- **Website:** https://greenhouse.capital/
- **Focus:** Primarily fintech -- LOW-MODERATE fit

## KEY RECOMMENDATIONS FOR AFRICA MARKET ENTRY

**Immediate:** Google for Startups Africa (Mar 18) + Tony Elumelu Foundation (Mar 1) + Flat6Labs (rolling) + Founders Factory Africa (rolling)

**Highest fit (monitor):** HealthTech Hub Africa, UNDP timbuktoo, Villgro Africa

**Positioning tips:**
- Emphasize AI triage for AI-focused programs
- Highlight HIPAA compliance as data privacy differentiator
- Frame multi-portal as government/public health integration capability
- Address doctor-to-patient ratio challenge (1:5,000)
