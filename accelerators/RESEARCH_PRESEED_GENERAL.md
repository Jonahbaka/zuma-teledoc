## TIER 1: TOP GENERAL ACCELERATORS (Highest Impact)

### 1. Y Combinator (YC)
- **Website:** https://www.ycombinator.com/apply
- **Investment:** $500K for 7% + incremental equity
- **Duration:** 3 months, in-person San Francisco
- **Acceptance:** ~1.5-2% from 20,000+ applicants/batch
- **2026 Timeline:**
  - Spring 2026 (X26): Late apps still accepted; on-time decisions by Mar 13
  - Summer 2026 (S26): Deadline ~May 2026
  - Fall 2026: Deadline ~Aug 2026
- **Healthcare portfolio:** 563+ healthcare, 144 healthtech, 95+ digital health companies
- **Fit:** EXCELLENT -- actively funding AI-native healthcare ops tools

### 2. Techstars AI Healthcare Baltimore
- **Website:** https://www.techstars.com/accelerators/baltimore-ai-health
- **Investment:** $220K ($20K for 5% + $200K SAFE)
- **Duration:** 13 weeks, in-person Baltimore
- **Partners:** Johns Hopkins + CareFirst BlueCross BlueShield
- **2026:** Apps open Mar 2; deadline Jun 10 for Fall 2026 cohort
- **Fit:** OUTSTANDING -- AI + healthcare specific

### 3. Techstars Healthcare (Permanente Medicine, DC)
- **Website:** https://www.techstars.com/accelerators/permanente-medicine
- **Investment:** $220K standard Techstars terms
- **Partners:** Kaiser Permanente

### 4. Techstars Northwestern Medicine Healthcare (Chicago)
- **Website:** https://www.techstars.com/accelerators/northwestern-medicine-healthcare
- **Investment:** $220K standard

### 5. 500 Global Flagship
- **Website:** https://500.co/founders/flagship
- **Investment:** $150K for 6% (convertible); pro-rata up to $500K
- **Duration:** 4 months, in-person Palo Alto
- **2026:** Batch 30 deadline Mar 31; Batch 31 deadline Aug 31
- **Fit:** GOOD -- industry-agnostic; global network

### 6. LAUNCH Accelerator (Jason Calacanis)
- **Website:** https://launch.co/apply
- **Investment:** $125K for 7%
- **Duration:** 14-15 weeks, remote-friendly
- **Cohort:** 7 startups each; rolling applications
- **Fit:** GOOD -- media exposure; 700+ investor access

## TIER 2: STRONG FIT SPECIALIZED PROGRAMS

### 7. Antler
- **Website:** https://www.antler.co/residency
- **Investment:** $100K-$190K for 10-12%
- **Duration:** 6-week residency
- **Location:** 30+ cities globally; rolling applications
- **Best for:** Solo founders seeking co-founders

### 8. Entrepreneur First (EF)
- **Website:** https://apply.joinef.com/
- **Investment:** Equity-free during ideation + $600K+ credits; ~$250K pre-seed investment (~10%)
- **Duration:** Two 12-week phases
- **Locations:** London, SF, Bangalore, Paris
- **Deadline:** General Spring 2026 by Mar 16
- **Best for:** Pre-team technical founders

### 9. Dreamit Healthtech
- **Website:** https://www.dreamit.com/healthtech
- **Investment:** Up to $150K for 6-9% (pre-seed)
- **Duration:** 14 weeks; rolling applications
- **Model:** Customer Sprints (pitch 15-25 health system execs) + Investor Sprints (20-30 VCs)
- **Fit:** EXCELLENT for B2B healthcare sales

### 10. MassChallenge HealthTech
- **Website:** https://masschallenge.org/programs-healthtech/
- **Investment:** Zero equity; non-dilutive prizes
- **Duration:** 4 months, Boston
- **Requirements:** <$10M raised, <$10M annual revenue
- **2026 apps:** Closed; watch for next cycle

### 11. Plug and Play Health
- **Website:** https://www.plugandplaytechcenter.com/industries/health
- **Investment:** Zero equity required; optional $50K-$250K (1-5%)
- **Duration:** 3 months; twice/year (20 startups each)
- **Partners:** Cleveland Clinic, J&J, Sanofi, Roche
- **Fit:** STRONG for enterprise partnerships

### 12. Creative Destruction Lab (CDL) Health
- **Website:** https://creativedestructionlab.com/streams/health/
- **Investment:** Zero equity, zero fees
- **Duration:** 9 months (Oct-Jun), sessions every 8 weeks
- **Location:** Toronto, Vancouver
- **Deadline:** ~Jul 2026 (estimated)
- **Fit:** GOOD -- extremely founder-friendly
