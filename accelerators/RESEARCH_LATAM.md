## TIER 1: HIGHEST FIT (LatAm-Specific, Active 2026)

### 1. Start-Up Chile (CORFO)
- **Website:** https://startupchile.org/en/
- **Location:** Santiago, Chile
- **Tracks:**
  - Build: 4 months, ~$18K equity-free grant
  - Ignite: 4 months, ~$32K equity-free grant
  - Growth: 8-12 months, up to $100K equity-free grant
- **Investment:** Equity-free grants
- **Deadline:** Multiple calls/year; most recent BIG 10 closed Nov 28, 2025
- **Fit:** EXCELLENT -- equity-free; healthtech is priority; includes 2-year work visa

### 2. Parallel18 (Puerto Rico)
- **Website:** https://parallel18.com/
- **Location:** San Juan, Puerto Rico (US territory)
- **Duration:** 5 months
- **Investment:** $100K via SAFE (shifted from grants in 2026)
- **Deadline:** Gen. 14 expected ~Apr/May 2026
- **Fit:** EXCELLENT -- US territory (HIPAA-friendly); BioLeap sub-program; Act 60 tax incentives

### 3. Google for Startups AI First (Latin America)
- **Website:** https://startup.google.com/programs/accelerator/ai-first/latin-america/
- **Duration:** 10-12 weeks
- **Investment:** Equity-free; up to $350K Google Cloud credits
- **Deadline:** Periodic windows throughout 2026
- **Fit:** EXCELLENT -- AI-first thesis matches AI triage; healthtech startups in prior cohorts

### 4. 500 Global (LatAm Program)
- **Website:** https://500.co/
- **Investment:** $300K for 10% via KISS; IDB Lab co-invested $2M
- **Duration:** 12-16 weeks
- **Deadline:** Flagship Batch 37 Q1 2026 open; LatAm cycles announced periodically
- **Fit:** STRONG -- healthtech is target vertical

### 5. Platanus Ventures
- **Website:** https://platan.us/
- **Location:** Remote-first (Mexico City + Santiago base)
- **Duration:** 12 weeks
- **Investment:** $200K SAFE for 5.5-7% equity
- **Deadline:** Rolling; reapplication encouraged
- **Requirements:** At least one technical co-founder
- **Fit:** STRONG

## WHY DOCTARX IS WELL-POSITIONED FOR LATAM

- **AI triage** maps to "AI First" thesis (Google, ScaleUp Labs)
- **HIPAA compliance** is a differentiator where most LatAm healthtech lacks robust compliance
- **Multi-portal system** enables B2B (hospitals, clinics, insurance)
- Massive market need: Colombia telemedicine adoption surged 7,000% post-COVID; ~50% of Mexican doctors use telemedicine
- Subscription model viable in LatAm economics vs. US per-visit pricing
